# Synopsis - Battle Zeque Den

Built from a read of the whole extracted script (no guide or wiki covers the story). Side-scrolling beat 'em up
(Arsys / Asmik, 1994): pick one of three heroines; six chapters; each chapter opens with her monologue on the map and
ends in a boss dialogue. Most boss lines are shared by all three heroines (the tool lists them as "to Rufu/Hamusu/
Kairu"), so they must read right for any of the three - all three are teenage girls.

## Premise (the attract-mode prologue)
An ancient prophet foretold: when the world is about to end, the pact that holds heaven and earth apart is broken;
heaven cries out and earth answers; people's hearts fall into turmoil and strife breaks out everywhere; and the sign
of doom begins when the two forces (気, ki) of light and darkness separate.
Each heroine dreams that darkness (Hamusu: black clouds) covers Mount Shumisen, the sacred mountain at the root of
all creation (森羅万象の根源). She sets out to climb it and see whether the end of the world is coming.

## The heroines
- **Rufu** (流風, 17): tomboyish wanderer, rough and breezy ("あたし", "アンタ", "~だぜ", "行くぜェ"); philosophises
  about life flowing like a river. Old friend of Byakko.
- **Hamusu** (波無寿, 16): the youngest, childish and cheerful, says "ボク"; whines and drags vowels ("~なァ",
  "~だよォ"). Born by the river of chapter 3; she grew up knowing its guardian, whom she calls "おっちゃん" (Uncle).
- **Kairu** (河意留, 18): calm, cultured and dignified; polite to strangers ("です", "~かしら", "~わね") but blunt
  when resolved ("あなたを倒します！"). Thinks in five-element terms (wood, earth, water, fire). She knows Suzaku
  of old and is on first-name terms with her.

## Chapters (element of each stage in Kairu's ch4 line: wood, earth, water, fire)
1. **Forest / pass (wood)** - boss **Seiryu** (青龍, Azure Dragon), a proud young warrior ("オレ") who lives only for
   the present and won't let anyone pass, whatever their reason.
2. **Underground cave (earth)** - boss **Koryu** (黄龍, Yellow Dragon), Seiryu's father: an old man speaking old-man
   archaic Japanese ("~じゃ", "~の", "覚悟せい"). "Some things you are happier not knowing."
3. **River (water)** - boss **Genbu** (玄武; never named in the dialogue), a crude, laughing brute ("ぐへへ",
   "おめえ") who mocks the fallen dragons. With Hamusu he is the gruff old family friend from her birthplace; he
   fights her rather than let her go on, and dies telling her Shumisen is still far away. She mourns him.
4. **Volcano / temple city (fire)** - first a **fire-clan guard** at the ruined temple: the city is Horaku, capital
   of the fire clan, which guarded the sacred peak Shumisen, and the clan's protector is the war god (闘神) Suzaku.
   The guard is a fatalist: everything is the will of the great god, even the clan's destruction. Defeated, he
   calls on Suzaku-sama to punish the fool. Then boss **Suzaku** (朱雀, Vermilion Bird), a mannered, mocking lady
   with old-fashioned courtesan-style speech ("~ござんせんか", "教えてしんぜましょう", "わたくし"), who calls the
   heroine "お嬢さん" (young lady). With Kairu, an old acquaintance ("河意留さん"); defeated, Suzaku warns Kairu that
   she is wide open to a trick, says she can't die in peace beaten by someone like her, and dies.
5. **Desert (metal)** - boss **Byakko** (白虎, White Tiger), a tough young woman ("あたし", "アンタ") born to guard
   Shumisen: "everything with form crumbles one day - that is fate." She is Rufu's friend; with Rufu she reveals
   she knows this fight is her fate. Defeated, she warns Rufu that the summit is full of evil ki, asks whether Rufu
   ever thought of her as a friend, thanks her ("る・ふ・う" spelled out) and dies.
6. **Summit of Shumisen** - final boss **Totetsu** (トウテツ, from 饕餮), "the one who devours desire". Calm,
   philosophical, condescending ("勇敢な娘だねおまえは"). Everything has a fixed fate; a wish beyond fate is called
   desire; every living thing wants to preserve itself forever, a wish too far, i.e. desire; humanity's foolish
   desire creates Totetsu through supernatural power. Destroying it won't undo everything.
   **Ending (late reveal):** what the heroine defeated was her own desire; she hasn't beaten Totetsu, only sealed
   its power for a while. It will return - sooner than expected, as long as humans stay as they are - stronger, and
   looks forward to fighting her again. "Until then, hone your strength and skill. Farewell!"

Chapter titles (cards, graphics text) and the profile screens are separate; profiles list name, birthday (South
Lenben calendar), age, height, B/W/H measurements and blood type.
